# Design System: Cute Island

## Overview

Cute Island is a compact black desktop surface for following local Agent activity. Its visual identity comes from the changing island silhouette, system typography, drawn icons, and restrained state colors. The companion settings window is organized by Agent, in the manner of a system-settings app: a sidebar of Agent and tool entries, grouped lists of rows on a graphite (dark) or cool gray (light) canvas, and green for enabled controls and the current page.

This document records the implemented system as of the October 30 redesign, which replaced the earlier functional-card settings window. The island itself, its state colors, geometry, motion, and its focus, managed, attention and docking behaviors are unchanged. Earlier settings rounds are recorded in the [settings surface brief](.impeccable/surfaces/src-renderer-src-settings-tsx.md).

**Key Characteristics:**

- A black floating island paired with settings that support light, dark and system appearance.
- Settings pages are destinations in a grouped sidebar: a home page, one page per Agent, the request-handling tools, then app settings.
- Grouped lists: a small label above a rounded card of divided rows, with an optional footnote below.
- Color attached to activity state, enabled controls and the current page; status dots always sit next to text.
- System sans typography with Chinese font fallbacks; one 13px body size with tighter supporting text.
- Drawn SVG icons, the island's own Agent marks, and a small three-dot island mark.

## Colors

The island retains its black identity. Settings use matched dark and light palettes; unsuffixed `settings-*` tokens are the dark palette and `settings-light-*` tokens are the light overrides of the same custom properties. State colors carry the main chromatic information.

### Primary

The settings accent is the existing success green in dark mode and a deeper green in light mode. It marks enabled switches, primary actions, the current navigation fill, available capabilities, `ok` status dots and values, and approved history entries. Primary-button ink is dark green in dark mode and white in light mode. The current page uses a solid selected fill (`settings-selected`) with white ink in both themes.

### Secondary

Running blue, thinking lavender, approval amber, success green and error coral keep their activity meanings. Settings have their own theme-aware blue focus token. Warning (`warn` status, unsaved drafts, missing credentials, pending requests) and error (blacklist tags, failed tests, destructive actions) each resolve text, surface and border per theme. These are semantic colors, not decoration.

### Neutral

The island is black with white text and three translucent white supporting roles (.74, .55 and .48). Dark settings step from a canvas (`#1c1c1e`) to a slightly lighter sidebar and a raised card surface; light settings use a cool gray canvas, a darker gray sidebar and white cards. Both palettes provide text, secondary text, muted text, a faint tone for non-text marks (chevrons, empty status dots, unavailable capability rings), inputs, row dividers (`line-soft`) and card edges (`line`).

`settings-faint` is never used for readable text. Muted text measures at least 4.89:1 against every settings background it sits on; the lowest pair is light muted on the light sidebar.

### Navigation tiles

Tool and app entries in the sidebar use small colored tiles: rules green, remote blue, history lavender, island black and general gray (`settings-tile-*`). They identify a destination, not a state, and never appear inside page content. Agent entries use the island's Agent badge colors instead.

## Typography

The font stack is the body token, including system UI and Chinese fallbacks; settings introduce no display family. The settings body is 13px/1.55. Row titles are 13px/550 and row descriptions 12px/1.5 in muted text. Group labels above cards are 12px/600 in muted text, and footnotes below cards are 11.5px. Page titles in the top bar are 17px/650; Agent and home pages instead open with a centered 21px/700 hero title. Master switches use a 14px/650 label. Buttons and inputs are 12.5px. Sidebar group headings are 11px/650, and counts use the command monospace face with tabular numerals.

The island keeps its denser type: compact titles 13.5px/650, expanded titles 16px/650, compact Agent names 11.5px at .82 opacity, and most state labels and timing metadata between 10px and 12px. Command content, paths and blacklist keywords use the command monospace role. Timestamps and counts use tabular numerals. Long sidebar labels and row values truncate; descriptions, paths and footnotes wrap.

## Layout

The native island sits inside a transparent host, initially aligned near the top edge. Its content remains within the viewport minus 16px. The rest, idle and compact forms are (92 × 12px), (126 × 36px) and (340 × 40px); expanded width is (380px), stacked width is (400px), and content-dependent heights cap at (520px). Docked forms are (72 × 16px) horizontally or (16 × 72px) vertically. Geometry and attention adjustments remain owned by Island.tsx.

The settings window fills the viewport with a fixed sidebar (236px) and an independently scrolling main column. Content is centered and capped at (680px), with padding (18px 30px 40px). A top bar holds the page title (on non-hero pages) and the save status. Groups are separated by 22px; group labels and footnotes are inset 13px so they align with row text. Rows are at least 46px tall with 10px 14px padding; dividers start 14px in from the card edge. A row's copy and its controls share one flex line and wrap below each other when space runs out.

At (760px) or less, content padding becomes 20px inline and remote fields become one column. At (700px) or less, the sidebar collapses to a 60px icon rail: labels, group headings and the footer hide, icons grow to 26px, counts become corner badges and status dots sit on the icon's corner. Display-mode choices become one column. At (460px) or less, content padding becomes 12px, rows use 11px inline padding, and pending and history rows may wrap. The native window opens at (1040 × 800px) with a minimum of (640 × 540px); the 390px layout supports browser preview rather than a separate mobile application.

## Elevation & Depth

The island floats with a tight contact shadow and wider ambient shadow, a faint white inset edge and a subtle top highlight. Error and approval states replace the inset edge with their attention color. Keyboard focus uses a stronger inset white edge. Exact values are captured in the sidecar.

Settings are nearly flat. Cards draw a 1px edge (`settings-line`) as a box shadow plus a subtle theme shadow; there are no nested shadows inside cards. An enabled master card switches to the enabled surface and border. The remote save bar is sticky at the bottom of the scroll area over a gradient of the canvas color. The help popover is the only floating panel. Focus outlines sit outside controls.

## Shapes

The island silhouette changes with its mode, using the corresponding frontmatter radius. Its session rows, circular Agent badges and pill buttons echo that form. Expanded capsules use a 32px radius, detail surfaces use 12px, and open stack rows use 20px inside the 28px stack.

Settings cards use 10px corners; buttons, inputs, selects, segmented controls and navigation items use 7px; sidebar tiles, Agent marks in rows, channel marks and blacklist tags use 6px. The home tile is 11px and the 60px hero mark is 17px. In settings, Agent badges become rounded squares; on the island they stay circular. Status dots, counts, pills and switches are fully rounded. Dividers are 1px. SVG settings icons use a (24 × 24) viewBox with round caps and joins, at a (1.6px) stroke (2px inside 22px tiles).
## Components

### Navigation

The sidebar starts with a home entry: the black island tile, the product name and a one-line subtitle that names the number of pending requests when there are any. Below it come three groups: Agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI), 处理方式 (自动规则, 远程接管, 处理记录) and 应用 (灵动岛, 通用). Each entry has a leading mark and a trailing indicator: a status dot (`ok`, `warn` or a hollow `off` ring) or a count. The current entry uses the solid selected fill with white ink; the dot and count invert to stay visible. Counts and update notices are drawn from attributes so each entry's text stays exactly its label; the state is announced through `aria-label`. The footer shows the version, or a green link when an update is available.

### Rows

Every page is built from the same row types inside a group card. A setting row has a title and an optional description on the left, with a switch, select, segmented control or buttons on the right. A link row is a full-width button with an optional leading mark, a title and description, a right-aligned value colored by tone, and a chevron; it navigates to another page or opens an editor. A capability row has a green check or a hollow dash, a title, and a line explaining either what it does or the single step that would enable it. Inline notes inside a card use warning or error text for blocked actions and Hook errors.

### Buttons and fields

Buttons are 28px tall with 7px corners. The secondary button uses the raised surface; the primary button uses the accent and is reserved for the one action that enables something (install, start, save). Destructive buttons keep the secondary surface with error-colored text. Disabled controls drop to .5 opacity. Inputs and selects are 28px with a 1px line border and the input surface; command, path and keyword inputs use the monospace face. All interactive elements share a 2px blue focus outline with a 2px offset, including disclosure summaries and segmented choices.

### Switches and master cards

The switch keeps its semantic role, linked label and description. The 34 × 20px track holds a 16px thumb that travels 14px when checked, with 150ms ease-out background and transform transitions. A master card (自动处理 on 自动规则, 远程接管 on its own page) combines a 14px switch row with a footer of a status dot, short state text and a link to the related page. When enabled, the card's surface and border switch to the enabled tokens. The rules footer also shows the handling order as small chips: 黑名单 → 规则 → 远程待办 or 原 Agent.

### Hero

Home and Agent pages open with a centered hero: a 60px mark, a 21px title, one muted sentence and a status pill colored by the same tone as the sidebar dot. The home mark is the island tile; Agent marks are the island's Agent badges enlarged.

### Blacklist

Keywords appear as red tags in the monospace face, each with an accessible remove button. Adding or removing one saves immediately through the shared preference normalization, so trimming and case-insensitive duplicates are handled in one place; a duplicate disables the add button and says so. Pasting a multi-line list adds one tag per line. Below the tags, 试一试 shows the verdict for a pasted command or question with a toned dot. It uses the same matcher as the hooks, and only reports the blacklist and approval rules, never execution success.

### Remote channels

Channels and other Codex connections are listed as link rows with their destination, user count, credential variable name and a value (unsaved, missing credential, service running or saved). Selecting one, or adding one, opens its editor in place with a back link; all editors stay mounted, so a draft survives switching rows or pages. A sticky bar at the bottom holds save, reload and the draft status. While takeover is running, a notice explains that channels are read-only and offers to turn takeover off.

### Mode choices

Each display mode is a card with a drawn preview of the black island over a desktop window and a native radio with its title and description. The managed preview shows the island faded. The selected card has a 2px accent ring. Display modes remain separate from automatic approval.

### Theme selection

通用 offers 浅色, 深色 and 跟随系统 as a segmented control made of native radios, so arrow keys still move the selection. There is no longer a theme switch in the sidebar. The saved preference defaults to system, including older configuration without a valid theme. System mode follows media-query changes; an explicit choice takes precedence. The renderer sets the resolved color scheme, and Electron nativeTheme keeps the native window background (`#1c1c1e` / `#f1f2f4`) matched to the canvas at startup, on change and on reopen. Appearance applies only to the configuration page; the island stays black.

### Activity and history

The island's small blue task badge has a compact icon and count; Agent badges preserve identity separately from activity state. Activity colors are accompanied by icons or text. On the settings home page, 等你处理 lists recorded approval and question requests using the island's attention rule, with no guesses from elapsed time. History rows show the Agent mark, the colored action and summary, the Agent name and a tabular time. The empty state is a centered card with two setup steps and routes to an Agent page and to 自动规则.

### Feedback and motion

The top bar's save status is a live region with a green or amber dot. Loading text appears while configuration is fetched, an inline error offers a retry, and the browser preview is labeled. Copy distinguishes connected approval channels from log observation, and an approval or answer from execution success.

The island preserves its geometry spring and state animations. Settings motion is limited to the switch and the disclosure chevron; changing page or appearance adds no decorative animation. Reduced-motion handling disables those transitions and the update progress transition; the island's geometry and opacity transitions reduce to a near-instant duration. Exact motion values are in the sidecar.

### Tray and window controls

The tray uses the operating system's native menu styling. Configuration and the current show/hide action form the first group; display mode and session recognition form the second; automatic approval and remote takeover remain direct checkbox shortcuts in the third. Exit is separated at the bottom. The display-mode submenu names its current mode. The tooltip summarizes window visibility, recognition, approval and takeover. Recognition disabled by the launch environment remains unchecked and unavailable; connection testing is named and temporarily disables the takeover switch.

In settings, 灵动岛 holds the display modes, session recognition, the visibility row (status text, state dot and one action), position recovery and the demonstration. Native show/hide events keep settings and the tray in sync, and position recovery also reveals a hidden window.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- Do preserve the black island silhouette, its state colors and the existing system font stack.
- Do build settings pages from groups, setting rows, link rows and capability rows rather than one-off layouts.
- Do describe what will actually happen to the next request, and when something is unavailable, name the one step that enables it.
- Do keep state colors attached to their meanings and pair every status dot with text.
- Do retain visible keyboard focus, linked control labels, disabled feedback and reduced-motion behavior.
- Do reuse the island's drawn Agent marks and icons.

### Don't

- Don't treat automatic approval as a display mode or imply that observing logs establishes an approval connection.
- Don't describe an approved permission or submitted answer as successful tool execution.
- Don't use `settings-faint` or navigation tile colors for text or state.
- Don't put counts or extra text into a navigation entry's visible label; use attributes and `aria-label`.
- Don't promote synthetic preview data, review screenshots or sidecar tonal ramps into product content or new visual tokens.
